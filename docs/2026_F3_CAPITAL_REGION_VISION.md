# 2026 F3 Capital Region Vision

## Vision

F3 Capital Region will align resources and mobilization efforts around local charities that reflect our mission — fostering in the Pax a clear sense that we are actively invigorating the communities around us through leadership, empowerment, mentorship, and service.

## 2026 Community Partners

• **Wesley Housing**

Provides subsidized housing and supportive services that help individuals and families build long-term stability and self-sufficiency.

https://wesleyhousing.org/

• **Kitchen of Purpose (La Cocina VA)**

Teaches culinary skills and entrepreneurship to empower individuals to build careers and pathways out of poverty.

https://www.kitchenofpurpose.org/

• **703 Warriors**

Peer-led veteran community focused on fitness, connection, and support for veterans navigating life after service.

https://www.arlcf.org/nonprofit/703-warriors/

• **Veterans on the Rise**

Provides mentorship, resources, and community support to help veterans regain purpose, stability, and direction post-service.

https://www.veteransontherise.org/

• **Travis Manion Foundation**

Delivers mentorship and leadership development in the legacy of a fallen Marine, instilling character, service, and responsibility.

https://www.travismanion.org/

## Flagship 2026 Initiative: F3 Capital Region Amazing Race

- **"Overnight" (6-Midnight) team ruck endurance challenge around Washington, DC**
- ~15-mile square route (Strava map forthcoming)
- Teams of 5 | 30-lb ruck plates
- Beat-down checkpoints every ~2.5 miles; complete to receive next map point
- Entry: $100 per team | Goal: 20 teams = $2,000 base + sponsorships
- Start & finish at the Marina | 6:00 PM start | ~6 hours
- Post-event bonfire and fellowship (ARC-style)
- Proceeds support all 2026 community partners
- Winning team receives 25% of total pot
